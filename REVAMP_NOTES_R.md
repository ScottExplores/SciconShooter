## Changes and files

- `services/revamp/theme/WorldTheme.ts`: ten palettes derived from N's actual
  sector identities, plus lime/ultraviolet Weekly Aurora. Authored graph aliases,
  bosses and mastery branches resolve to the same sector. Danger stays warm and
  projectiles keep their existing colors. UI accents exceed 4.5:1 contrast on the
  corresponding dark surfaces.
- `WorldBackdrop.ts`: N's existing scenery pipeline also renders Weekly Aurora;
  weekly stage IDs supply private cosmetic seeds. Campaign scenery is unchanged.
- `themeUi.ts`: stable inherited CSS variables and a guarded, disposable
  `qa=1&uiAudit=1` result-theme preview. No profile or reward writes.
- `spritePixels.ts`, `SpriteIdleAtlas.ts`, `SectorSpritePresentation.ts`:
  four baked energy/facet frames per loaded source pose, selective enemy armor
  palette swaps, inner-edge pulses, pre-baked additive glow and four masked
  metal-sweep frames. Black outlines, neutral metal, source alpha, authored
  steering poses, ship proportions and collision geometry remain authoritative.
  Player/wing hues retain their ship identity. The existing P/I thrusters,
  bobbing, authored strips and receipt-owned hit flash remain active.
- `BackgroundShockwave.ts`: one High-only radial displacement pass for large
  kills, boss parts/phases and wave warps. It affects only the background, lasts
  27 simulation presentation frames, and is absent in Low/Medium/reduced motion.
  Combat sprites, bullets and telegraphs never pass through this filter.
- `styles/revamp-theme.css`: HUD rails, campaign nodes/briefing, result accent
  text and glows, inset running lights, pulsing crystals, isolated crown-gem tint
  and slow masked metal shimmer. No result-component edits or home restructuring.
- `tests-revamp/R/`: theme resolution/contrast, integer pixel frames, real Pixi
  atlas ownership/context recovery, pooled-view cleanup, motion gates, shader
  receipt lifecycle and offline gallery/lifecycle runner tests.
- `tests/campaignMapUi.test.cjs`: two existing source contracts follow the new
  shared resolver and root attributes; route/layout assertions remain intact.
- `tools-revamp/qa/theme-gallery.mjs`, `theme-lifecycle.mjs`,
  `theme-shockwave.mjs`: offline authored
  combat, explicit synthetic result presentation, live motion/quality switches,
  pause, actual WebGL loss/restoration and a natural boss-kill shader pulse.
  No QA gate was lowered.

## Q integration contract

The App root supplies `data-world-theme` and `--world-accent`, `--world-secondary`,
`--world-danger`, `--world-glow`, `--world-ink`, `--world-surface`, `--world-metal`,
`--world-energy-hue`, `--world-gem-hue`. Existing result selectors are supported
in the new stylesheet. New Q panels can use:

- `[data-theme-accent]` / `.theme-accent`: accent text.
- `.frame-anim`: running lights on `::after`, positioned relative to the panel.
- `.frame-gem` / `[data-frame-gem]`: opacity pulse.
- `.frame-metal-shimmer` with `--frame-shimmer-mask: url(...)`: masked `::before`
  sweep; apply to a positioned art layer. Avoid assigning both adapters to a
  pseudo-element already owned by a component.

All decoration is inset and pointer-free. The original gold/steel frame remains;
the crown overlay recolors only the central crystal. Reduced motion stops the
new CSS motion and removes metal sweeps. Q's later centering checks still need
to run on the combined branch.

## Budgets, tuning and limits

- One 2048² nearest atlas (16 MiB GPU), max 288 packed entries and 48 source
  poses. Role reservations prioritize enemy preparation. A maximum of one source
  pose is baked per render on first use; animation subsequently selects cached
  textures with no canvas readback or frame upload. Originals/CPU-backed frames
  survive atlas overflow/context loss. Only derived resources are destroyed.
- Cosmetic rasters use integer nearest reduction to at most 128 pixels per side,
  while texture logical bounds and existing actor scales are preserved. This is
  not a claim of byte-identical native source resolution or integer transforms
  for existing P/I banking/bobbing. Unsupported/over-budget poses retain their
  authored artwork and animation. Campaign assets are not all preloaded at once.
- Idle cadence: seven simulation frames, twelve in Low; reduced motion selects
  one stable frame. P/I already owns thruster/flap/steering motion; R adds energy
  and facet animation rather than inventing new collision-affecting geometry.
- Extra quads are limited to pickup/elite outlines/glows and ship/boss metal.
  Low hides them; High alone plays the 24-frame metal sweep every 330 frames.
  Generic assembled bosses select up to four large visible art pieces, refreshed
  every 30 simulation frames. Changed/destroyed/retired owners are handled safely.
- Shockwave texture: 64² CPU-backed displacement map; one resolution-1 background
  pass, at most 390×844, max 27 frames. No per-entity filter stacks or blur bloom.
- CSS: running light 8 seconds, gem pulse 3.8 seconds, metal sweep 9 seconds with
  a long quiet interval. Colors live in `WorldTheme.ts`; masks/alpha/timing in CSS.
- N's lookup remains authoritative for campaign identity. Standalone optional
  branch/replay campaign forks that remove sector metadata retain its existing
  fallback; those separate entry points need source-sector context if they are
  expected to inherit a parent world's palette. Canonical campaign graph,
  mastery-node and boss IDs are covered by the resolver tests.
- Read-only QA: `__sciconSpriteThemeQA.snapshot()` and
  `__sciconShockwaveQA.snapshot()`, only with `qa=1`. The separate result preview
  requires UI-audit mode and no active wallet.
