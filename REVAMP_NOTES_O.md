# O — Interactive bosses

## Delivered behavior

The fifteen C major profiles now use independently destructible outer and inner armaments, then an exposed core. Visible fittings have HP strips and intact/cracked/smoking/wreck states; each weapon owns its attack and stops when destroyed. Breaks cancel committed hazards, award real coins, create an explosion and grant a quiet stagger. Centered autofire routes shielded hull damage to the nearest remaining fitting, so a player cannot get stuck behind the new armor gates.

Physical shutters expose a recovery weak point (1.85× open damage; 0.8× closed). Shell leaves separate and inner hardware emerges, using four palette/silhouette motifs over Scott's preserved original hulls. Phase two adds warned edge machinery; phase three adds outward beam sweeps or chevrons with a central route. Hangar doors visibly deploy two existing drone sprites with bounded ownership and safe deploy/exit collision. The entrance combines a 72-frame cubic fly-in, alert sound, landing shake and existing name/epithet card. Major death uses the actual common A kill path: a 12-burst chain over 1.6 seconds, retained hull fragments, flash/debris, 24 half-rate simulation updates and final coin rain. Mini deaths retain their authored animation and elite blast/hit-stop; they do not claim major-only slow motion. O adds part-break effects and death collection invulnerability.

All ten campaign mini identities participate at an appropriate scale. Eight authored mini encounters retain their guardians, paired members, handed art and signature choreography, with a destructible support relay/generator and a two-stage shell. Their original weapons retain their authored firing sockets; the new relay disables the linked support pattern. Bottleneck Drone receives warned fan/ring sentences and fires directly from its new cannon. Paywall Wall retains its destructible marching segments and fires only from surviving, visibly charging vents. B's opening Peer Reviewer keeps its first-minute teaching shield.

No existing art was replaced or deleted. No new raster assets, APIs, external writes, pushes or deployments. Top-level `api/*.js` remains 12. No City Mechanical/scooter work.

## Tuning

Major outer parts each use 14% of hull max HP, inner parts 12%. Part collision radius is 19. Core opening controls the exposed hull damage window; the stored core radius is metadata, not an additional aiming hitbox. Layer integrity milestones remain 66% and 33%; transition 60 frames, break stagger 72. Source-specific mount ratios keep Crownclaw/Oculus/Hammerhead fittings on painted chassis; Hammerhead uses .68/.72 outer/inner Y ratios. Core shutters move over 18 simulation frames. Lower sockets receive 108-frame warnings; arena warnings are 84 frames. Other warnings remain at least 36 frames (0.6 seconds). Gameplay always uses the same 390×844 world and fixed-step timing.

Mini parts each use 9% of hull max HP, break/phase quiet windows 48 frames. Support Y is .68 for Bottleneck/Railspine/Gridline, .74 for Convoy and .90 otherwise; these source-specific anchors avoid transparent sprite margins. Wall vents warn for 48 frames, with a narrow pair after half the segments are gone. Queued mini rounds carry a weapon role and cancellation generation. Existing hazard activation additionally requires a fresh accepted warning receipt, preventing an old suppressed warning from producing a delayed beam.

Escort timing: 42-frame deployment, active until frame 144, then exit and retirement at 180. No independent firing; only visibly active escorts may ram. Major death invulnerability is 120 frames, mini 72, individual wall segment 18. Existing final encounter reward code owns coin rain; O explicitly creates only part-break coins.

## Constraints and handoff

The exposed core grants its damage multiplier across hull hits during the open window; this pass does not add a separate small core aiming target. The phase transformation moves procedural shell leaves and reveals inner machinery over the original sprites. It is not a new set of fully repainted second-form hull sprites. Arena changes use a local retained overlay; no job N background API is required. Major escort waves deliberately avoid independent bullets so their own complete collision lifecycle stays readable. Bounded natural autoplay observations are not claims that every boss was defeated naturally.

**The requested 30 FPS acceptance gate is unmet.** The unchanged baseline also fails the smoke threshold, but the final measurements do not establish that O has no performance regression. Existing Gridline west-member name text clips the canvas edge; the new fittings remain within the portrait arena. Common completion/coin feedback also overlaps controls/text in the production playthrough. The DOM overflow audit does not detect these Pixi-text occlusions. The factory checklist retains its unchecked stricter art-proof requirements rather than treating the browser roster run as their substitute.
