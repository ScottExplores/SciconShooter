SCICON SHOOTER · ANDROID

Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy covers the SciCon Shooter app on Google Play, package xyz.sciconshooter.play, published by Scicon Shooter. The app is a Trusted Web Activity: a thin Android shell that opens the game at https://scicon-play.vercel.app/. It is not the older native build. Contact the developer at sciconshooter@gmail.com.

You can play without an account. The Play app has no advertising, no login, and nothing to buy. Progress is stored on your device in the app's web storage. Feedback and bug reports are sent only when you submit them.

Data stored on your device

The game saves mission progress, earned tokens, ships, upgrades, daily gifts, achievements, and settings in storage on the device (the browser storage of the Trusted Web Activity). These saves stay on the device. Clearing the app's storage removes them. Your device or Android backup settings may separately control operating-system backups.

Optional pilot name and scores

If you submit a score, we receive the pilot name you typed, the score, and ordinary connection details needed to store the leaderboard entry. You do not have to submit a score to play. Leaderboard names may be shown to other players.

Optional feedback and bug reports

If you submit a report from the menu, we receive its category, summary and details, a randomly generated report reference and session identifier, the app version and timestamp. You do not have to enter an email address. If you choose to include diagnostics, the report can also include gameplay state, mission, selected ship, score, screen dimensions, connection state, and relevant technical details available to the form. Diagnostics are used to investigate reliability and gameplay issues.

Report text may contain personal information you choose to write. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information. There is no background report-upload queue.

Usage measurements

The Play site uses Vercel Web Analytics for cookieless page views, and the game may record anonymous usage events and bounded crash diagnostics. Gameplay analytics use a random device-local identifier, stored only as a one-way server hash, plus foreground-time samples. They are not used for advertising. The app does not use the Android advertising ID.

Online connections and service providers

Research proposals and their images are fetched over HTTPS so you can read them. Those connections expose ordinary connection information, such as IP address and request details, to the services handling the request. Hosting and security services may process connection logs for delivery, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. The research feed is informational.

The site is hosted on Vercel. Leaderboard entries and submitted reports may be stored in Supabase. Manual reports may be emailed to the developer through Resend. These providers process information to operate these services. We do not sell this data or use it for targeted advertising.

Google Play may independently process installation, device, crash, or diagnostic information under Google's policies and your device settings.

What this app does not do

The Play app does not ask you to connect an external account, does not offer purchases, and does not include an advertising SDK. Links that leave the app, such as a ResearchHub proposal page, are separate sites with their own privacy practices.

Retention and security

On-device progress remains until you clear app storage or remove the app. Submitted reports, leaderboard entries, and support correspondence are retained for the leaderboard, support, bug investigation, and abuse prevention. The current service does not automatically erase reports after a fixed number of days. Limited records may need to be retained for security or legal obligations, and provider backups may remain until their normal retention cycles expire.

Network requests use HTTPS. Access to stored reports and support mail is restricted to the developer and the service providers involved in operating support. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Delete data or make a privacy request

On-device progress: use Android Settings → Apps → SciCon Shooter → Storage → Clear storage. This removes local progress and settings and cannot be undone by the developer. Manage any operating-system backup separately in your Android or Google backup settings.

Submitted reports or leaderboard entries: email sciconshooter@gmail.com with the subject “SciCon Shooter Android data deletion”. Include your report reference, if available, or the pilot name, approximate date, and score. We may ask for limited information to verify which record is yours. You may also request access to or correction of information you submitted. We will explain any information we must retain and why.

The app does not create an online user account, so there is no account to close.

Age range

The app is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information through a report or a pilot name, contact us so we can locate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. The effective date appears above. Questions or requests: sciconshooter@gmail.com.

SciCon Shooter is community-built and is not an official ResearchHub product.
