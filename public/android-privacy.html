SCICON SHOOTER · ANDROID

Privacy policy

Effective September 8, 2026

This policy covers the native Scicon Shooter Android app, published by Scicon Shooter, including its Android test versions. Contact the developer at sciconshooter@gmail.com.

You can play without an account or cryptocurrency wallet. Native progress is stored on your device. Feedback and bug reports are sent only when you submit them. The current native version has no advertising SDK, advertising-ID access, or real-money coin purchases.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your mission progress, earned coins, ships, upgrades, daily rewards and settings in app storage. These local saves are not submitted as verified website scores and are not synchronized with website accounts or wallet balances. Local save backups help recover damaged save files. Your device or Android backup settings may separately control operating-system backups.

Optional feedback and bug reports

If you submit a report from the More menu, we receive its category, summary and details, a randomly generated report reference and session identifier, the app version and timestamp. You do not have to enter an email address. If you choose to include diagnostics, the report can also include gameplay state, mission/wave, selected ship, score/lives, screen dimensions, connection state and relevant technical details available to the form. Diagnostics are used to investigate reliability and gameplay issues, not advertising.

Report text may contain personal information you choose to write. Please do not include passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, payment details, medical information or other sensitive information. The app and service redact common secret formats, but redaction cannot guarantee removal of every sensitive value. There is no background report-upload queue in the native app.

Online connections and service providers

Research proposals and their images are fetched over HTTPS from our website service and content-image providers. Those connections expose ordinary connection information, such as IP address and request details, to the services handling the request. Hosting and security services may process connection logs for delivery, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. The research feed is informational, not medical advice.

Our website infrastructure uses Vercel for hosting and Supabase for report storage. Manual reports are processed by Resend for email delivery to the developer's Gmail inbox. These providers process information to operate these services. Reports are not published as public posts. We do not sell report data or use it for targeted advertising.

Google Play may independently process installation, device, crash or diagnostic information under Google's policies and your device settings. The native app does not include a separate advertising or background gameplay-analytics SDK.

Links to the website and other services

Some buttons open the Scicon Shooter website, ResearchHub or other external pages in your browser. These are separate services with their own privacy practices. Visiting them does not connect a wallet to the native app or synchronize your native progress. Optional website wallet, competition and onchain features are separate from the native earned-coin game.

Retention and security

Local progress remains until you clear app storage or remove it. Submitted reports and support correspondence are retained for support, bug investigation and abuse prevention; the current service does not automatically erase reports after a fixed number of days. We handle deletion requests as described below. Limited records may need to be retained for security or legal obligations, and provider backups may remain until their normal retention cycles expire.

Network requests use HTTPS with certificate validation. Access to stored reports and support mail is restricted to the developer and the service providers involved in operating support. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Delete data or make a privacy request

On-device progress: use Android Settings → Apps → Scicon Shooter → Storage → Clear storage. This removes local progress and settings and cannot be undone by the developer. Manage any operating-system backup separately in your Android/Google backup settings.

Submitted reports: email sciconshooter@gmail.com with the subject “Scicon Shooter Android data deletion”. Include your report reference, if available, or enough information to locate the report, such as its approximate date and summary. Do not send passwords or wallet recovery phrases. We may ask for limited information to verify which record is yours. You may also request access to or correction of information you submitted. We will explain any information we must retain and why.

The native app does not create an online user account, so there is no native account to close. Deleting native app data does not delete records held by separately visited websites or public blockchains.

Age range

The app is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information through a report, contact us so we can locate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the native app's data practices change. The effective date appears above. Questions or requests: sciconshooter@gmail.com.
