# Organic Growth Plan

## Positioning

Scicon Shooter is not an official ResearchHub product unless that relationship is confirmed. Use “community-built” and “powered by ResearchHub proposal data.” The distinctive promise is participation: a good arcade run can influence which research proposal receives a transparent community allocation.

## Account draft

- Name: Scicon Shooter
- Handle ideas: `@SciconShooter`, `@PlayScicon`, `@SciconOnBase`
- Bio: `Play. Win the week. Choose what research gets funded. Community-built arcade game on Base, powered by active ResearchHub proposal data.`

No account should be created and nothing should be posted without approval.

## Weekly content loop

- Sunday after noon Pacific: new competition, prize amount, and one featured eligible proposal theme.
- Wednesday: current leader card, clearly labeled temporary.
- Friday: active-proposal roundup and gameplay clip.
- Sunday before noon Pacific: final countdown to the weekly cutoff.
- Sunday after finalization: champion announcement and claim status.
- After selection: proposal selected, not “funded.”
- After transaction confirmation: transparent funding result with explorer link.

## Launch thread draft

1. `Scicon Shooter is live: Play. Win the week. Choose what research gets funded.`
2. `Anyone can play instantly. A wallet is optional until you want to own a verified Mission Record or claim a finalized championship.`
3. `The Sunday-noon-to-Sunday-noon Pacific leaderboard locks at the weekly cutoff. Temporary leaders are never minted as champions.`
4. `The finalized winner chooses an active ResearchHub proposal for the community allocation. We publish the result and transaction transparently.`
5. `Built on Base, community-built, and open source: https://sciconshooter.xyz`

## First month

- Week 1: launch, control tutorial clip, proposal explainer, first final countdown.
- Week 2: champion story, selected research summary, game-polish clip, player score cards.
- Week 3: behind-the-scenes integrity thread, open-source invitation, Base builder update.
- Week 4: month recap with starts, completed runs, verified scores, wallet attachments, shares, and funding outcomes.

## Partnerships

Prioritize permission-based outreach to ResearchHub community members, proposal authors, Base builders, indie game communities, and onchain game curators. Share useful artifacts and results; do not mass-tag or spam.
