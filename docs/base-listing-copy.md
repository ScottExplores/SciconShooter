## Tagline

## Short description

Compete in a Base-native arcade shooter. The finalized weekly champion selects an active ResearchHub proposal for the community funding allocation.

## Full bio

Scicon Shooter is a community-built Base-native arcade game where players compete for the weekly high score. The finalized weekly champion directs a community funding allocation toward an active ResearchHub proposal. Anyone can play without a wallet, and players can optionally mint a truthful onchain record of a verified run.
