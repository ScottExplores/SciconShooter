Full description:

SciCon Shooter is a portrait arcade flight game about open science. Steer a research ship through waves of drones, clear campaign missions, and face the bosses from the game's own art.

What you can do
- Play the campaign and return to missions you have cleared
- Fight bosses and replay them from the Boss Archive
- Open Boss Lab to practice a boss
- Earn ships and hangar upgrades by playing
- Claim daily gifts and achievements stored on this device
- Read ResearchHub research proposals
- Change the audio and send feedback or a bug report

Progress stays on your device. There is no account and nothing to buy. There are no ads.

SciCon Shooter is community-built and is not an official ResearchHub product.
