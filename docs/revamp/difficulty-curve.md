## Model and limits

Regular wave authored pressure index is **Σ (spawn count × weighted role threatCost × fire-rate multiplier)**. Fire-rate multiplier is max(1, 3 / authored attackIntervalSeconds) × burstCount; never-fire hulls retain contact threat 1. Weighted enemy alternatives are probabilities, not simultaneous extra hulls. Wave totals below come from actual campaign events, not the budget caps. Budget caps include the declared pack ceiling and cannot establish actual pressure.

The mission target is the **mean regular formation encounter**, so a long wave with two squadron beats is comparable with two separate waves. Mission 1 deliberately compresses its three formations and mini-boss into one continuous 50-second wave. Its raw regular wave threat is 24, while its encounter mean is 8. From that base, four 7% steps are followed by a 10% sector reset: 8 × (1.07^4 × 0.90)^(sector−1) × 1.07^(slot−1). Optional routes use their graph depth, not array order 51–53. Within missions the factors are 0.85 / 1.00 / 1.10 / 1.25 / 0.70 / 1.10; a 0.70 recovery wave follows each checkpoint mini-boss. Major-boss lead-ins average 1.00 and end in the existing sector boss.

Expected power is an **authoring estimate**, not live telemetry: 1000 × 1.045^(mission−1); expected focused DPS is 4.8 × power/1000. Authored regular HP = expected DPS × target TTK (light 0.48 s, standard 0.65 s, armor 0.95 s, elite 1.10 s). The table assumes sustained hits, earned pickups and no paid upgrades; shield flank time, teleports, invulnerability, travel and aim loss add time. Actual mobile/desktop telemetry must calibrate this estimate. startingDifficulty and wave difficultyMultiplier remain 1 in these sectors to avoid applying an unrelated HP/fire/contact multiplier on top of the authored curve. Enemy contact damage remains 1. Difficulty tiers still apply their existing runtime rules.

Mini-boss manifest costs and major-boss encounters are reported separately because their attack timelines and health are not equivalent to regular-enemy firing cadence; they are not hidden inside the 7% claim. Major boss mechanics and HP are preserved for job C. This is a regular-enemy pressure curve, not a claim that complete boss fights differ by precisely 7%.

## Runtime cadence and the meaning of the index

The 7% figure is an **authored pressure index**, not a claim of 7% more live projectiles or player deaths. Runtime now consumes each role's authored interval: simple shooters fire on their own cadence; snipers include the 0.8-second charge in their shot period; mines, teleporters and carriers use role-specific period ratios. Diver intervals scale hold/recovery and flight pace, while the 0.8-second warning remains fixed. Single/trio peel selection, route length, entry/assembly, deaths, capped carrier broods, safe-spawn rejection and the bullet cap all lower observed output. The curve costs account for these role differences as design weights; autoplay measurements remain necessary to fit those weights to actual difficulty.

## Introduction and pacing

Mission 1: swoop begins at 0 s, guaranteed projectile-upgrade is authored at 6 s, divers enter at 10 s, magnet drops at 18 s, a four-ship reinforcement train enters at 20 s, Peer Reviewer warning begins at 28.8 s and enters at 30 s. The shared runtime hook preserves authored time instead of releasing later events early when the screen clears. One continuous wave avoids normal between-wave departure sequences. Clear-after-duration still requires surviving enemies to be defeated: 50 seconds is the planned combat beat, not a forced victory timer. The engine finishes ordinary waves as soon as their scheduled combat is exhausted and the field is clear; 19/24-second wave durations are scheduling windows, not minimum waits. Full mission length depends on kills and between-wave flight, and must be measured in autoplay rather than inferred by summing those windows. The pickups use zero-coin recovery events for immediate one-shot emission; the legacy repeating-powerup event delays its first spawn by its interval. Pickup arrival at the player's lane depends on descent/collection. Mobile autoplay exposed dead air after the second group, so the 20-second train repeats the established diver mechanic. Its encounter index 8 preserves the mission mean: (6 + 10 + 8) / 3 = 8. Entries release at 0.24 seconds (trains 0.26) to separate the existing hull art. Opening divers hold only 0.9 seconds before their first peel so the lesson occurs before an accurate player clears the group. Peer Reviewer uses 120 hull HP plus a separately breakable 8-HP shield; this was calibrated from the real engine replay rather than inferred from regular-enemy DPS. Wing joins, results, unlocks and boss presentation belong to the other revamp jobs.

New ideas are introduced at S1M1 Diver, S1M2 Escort Pair, S1M3 Shield Bearer, S1M4 Splitter, S2M1 Mine Layer, S2M2 Sniper, S2M3 Teleporter, S2M4 Carrier. Boss missions and sector 3 remix already learned ideas. Existing sprites and all later-sector authored content remain intact. Eight entry paths are selected from the shared revamp spline library; side/bottom variants begin only after the basic top-entry lesson. Each role has one declared pack and event choreography can vary its spline without ambiguous role bindings.

## Tuning and regeneration

- `services/revamp/curve/difficultyCurve.ts`: mission growth/reset, DPS/TTK estimates and cadence formula.
- `services/revamp/curve/authorCampaign.mjs`: roster costs/introduction, eight spline recipes, rhythm factors, encounter counts/cadence and power-up timing.
- `game-config/campaign-escalation-base.json`: refreshed spawn baselines for generated missions; stale removed-wave keys are pruned so an old density pass cannot double the new counts.
- `node tools-revamp/validate-campaign.mjs` validates the complete campaign with the repository validator.
- `node --test tests-revamp/B/curve*.test.mjs` checks ramp/reset, actual pressure, introduction, timings, TTK, reproducibility and role/pack integrity.
