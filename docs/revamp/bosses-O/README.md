# Job O boss evidence

The [Gatekeeper Prime benchmark package](gatekeeper-prime/boss-design.md) records the original reference, retained assets, added hardware pivots, attack ownership and deterministic proof witnesses. It is a review package for the requested enhancement of fifteen existing major profiles and ten mini identities, not a claim that every stricter Boss Factory gate passed.

- [Asset inventory](gatekeeper-prime/asset-usage.json): original Gatekeeper frames and handed arms retained; no new raster exports or atlases.
- [Rig and pivot manifest](gatekeeper-prime/rig-pivot-manifest.json): simulation-owned sockets, display dimensions, shutter motion, and reviewed source-specific major/mini mount corrections.
- [Attack mapping](gatekeeper-prime/attack-to-sprite-map.json): owning parts, real warning durations, movement rules, cleanup and named proofs.
- [Representative route witnesses](gatekeeper-prime/proof-samples.json): fixed inputs, source hashes, route hashes and clearance; full coverage remains in the O tests.
- [Visual review](gatekeeper-prime/visual-verification.md) and [review checklist](gatekeeper-prime/review-checklist.md): inspected screenshots, limitations and remaining evidence.
- [Final-build phone review](final-visual-review.md): inspected pacing-corrected roster images, separately labelled from older diagnostics and performance results.
- [Recorded motion review](final-visual-review.md#sampled-recorded-motion-review) and [hashed media manifest](/workspace/scicon-revamp/qa/O-bosses/media-review/manifest.json): sampled transformations and finite death progression for four bosses. Crownclaw and Helios use added [15.52-second](/workspace/scicon-revamp/qa/O-bosses/beam-forced-frontier-crownclaw/frontier-crownclaw-god-forced/defeat-complete.mp4) and [16-second](/workspace/scicon-revamp/qa/O-bosses/beam-forced-helios-clawcarrier/helios-clawcarrier-god-forced/defeat-complete.mp4) excerpts through removal; their original eight-second excerpts end during collapse.
- [UI visual review](ui-visual-review.md): representative phone/result/desktop-shell screenshots accompanying the 234-case, zero-overflow audit.

The canonical runtime remains `GameStage → GameCanvas → GameEngine → PixiGameRenderer`. QA enters that production path through the explicit Boss Lab roster. The task's campaign roster, procedural-art preference and visible warning-line requirements take precedence over the skill's single-boss sequencing, sandbox-only and hidden-guide defaults. No external art service, asset replacement or deployment is involved.
