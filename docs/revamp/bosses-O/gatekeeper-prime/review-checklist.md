# Job O review checklist

This checklist distinguishes implemented/tested scope from the stricter new-boss factory artifacts. Unchecked evidence is not a request to pause the user's authorized roster work.

- [x] Gatekeeper Prime is the named reference; complete before screenshot and original neutral frame have hashes.
- [x] Original art is retained. New retained procedural surfaces, ownership and asset decisions are inventoried.
- [x] Rig/socket manifest includes interaction sizes, display sizes, handed mini rotation and source-specific major/mini anchors.
- [x] Corrected production Prism/Crownclaw/Oculus screenshots were opened and inspected at native capture resolution.
- [x] Pacing-corrected Gatekeeper wall, sweep-warning and hangar frames were opened and documented in the [final-build review](../final-visual-review.md); older captures remain diagnostic.
- [x] All ten mini identities were visually scanned; all eleven source-specific mount/palette corrections were rechecked on the mounted build. These short opening diagnostics do not replace final natural runs or later-phase evidence.
- [x] Four corrected-build forced lifecycle PNG sets were inspected through active walls/sweeps, hangar/core openings and defeat/removal. Four sets of transformation/arena/escort/defeat MP4s are linked; sampled motion findings and failed mobile performance acceptance are documented separately.
- [x] All fifty required natural normal/god cases are captured with scoped build provenance. Six pass; forty-four fail only the ≥30 FPS threshold. No natural major defeat or full complete flag is claimed.
- [x] Damage states, independent part HP, shutter positions, paired-mini cleanup and retained display-object behavior have focused tests.
- [x] Major attacks, arena patterns and summons have explicit state owners, warnings and lifecycle mappings.
- [x] Representative deterministic witnesses record 3 px uncertainty and at least 6 px clearance; full matrix coverage is separately named.
- [ ] Formal same-center/equal-height opacity comparison in Boss Sandbox.
- [ ] One complete key-pose contact sheet with open/closed, maximum articulation, firing, phase two, stagger and death.
- [ ] Five exact preparation/telegraph/first-beat/peak/recovery captures for every benchmark attack.
- [x] Four final recordings inspected at two frames per second: outer break → inner exposure is visible in all four; defeats reach removal in the original Gatekeeper/Prism clips and added Crownclaw/Helios excerpts. Sampling does not establish smoothness, exact slow-motion timing or human fairness.
- [ ] Full-roster ≥30 median FPS acceptance. Current result fails in forty-four of fifty selected natural cases.
- [x] Four final wide combat PNGs compared with phone captures: 1280×800 DPR 1 versus 390×844 DPR 2, each retaining the 390×844 simulation world. Wide FPS passes do not clear the mobile gate.

Task-defined scope exceptions: campaign enhancement of the entire roster proceeds in parallel; no new raster components are required; visible warning lines are explicitly required. Existing four C attack families remain shared and are not recast as three bespoke core attacks plus a unique painted signature. No Director or private Runner files were changed, and there is no deployment.
