## One-sentence pitch

Scicon Shooter is a free DeSci-inspired arcade game on Base that converts repeat gameplay and weekly competition into optional onchain ownership and transparent community-directed support for eligible science proposals.

## Problem

Scientific funding is difficult for ordinary people to discover or participate in, while many DeSci products begin with wallets, tokens, and financial concepts before giving users a reason to care. At the same time, most games do not give player attention a transparent public-purpose outcome.

## Solution

Scicon Shooter starts with a familiar arcade loop: anyone can play immediately without a wallet, compete for a verified weekly score, and return for new ships, waves, and challenges. Players may optionally mint a truthful Mission Record on Base. The finalized weekly champion can select an eligible ResearchHub proposal for the community RSC funding-credit allocation. Proposal selection and confirmed funding are recorded as separate events and reported transparently.

This creates an acquisition path from gaming communities into onchain participation and scientific discovery without requiring players to begin as crypto or science experts.

## Sustainable model

Keep core gameplay free. Potential revenue sources are optional cosmetic or record mints, sponsored science challenges, branded ships or missions that fit the game, and community sponsorship of transparent allocation pools. Do not launch a project token merely to fit a funding category.

## Word-of-mouth loop

1. A gaming creator or regular player shares a score or ship.
2. Friends play without needing a wallet.
3. The weekly competition introduces a real eligible science proposal.
4. The proposal author or scientist can explain the research in plain language through a short mission briefing.
5. The game publishes the selected proposal and, later, the confirmed allocation receipt.
6. Players and scientists have a concrete outcome worth sharing outside their usual communities.
