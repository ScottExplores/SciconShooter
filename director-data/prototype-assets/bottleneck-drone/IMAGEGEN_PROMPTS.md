## Canonical design prompt summary

Create a revised M03 Bottleneck Drone using the previous containment-drone identity and Umbra Needlewing only as a pixel-detail benchmark. Use a short connected navy/gunmetal checkpoint-regulator silhouette, large readable armor shapes, one cyan containment lens, two blunt jointed clamps, two small dorsal sensors, tiny amber accents, and a visible lower pulse emitter. Keep the sprite intentionally simple, symmetric, opaque, hard-edged, and centered on a flat `#ff00ff` key with generous safety margins.

## Five-state animation prompt summary

Create five equal cells: neutral; sensor-only idle scan; jointed clamp closure and lens focus; lower-emitter fire/recoil; vent-and-reset recovery. Lock the central hull, root, scale, palette, and proportions. Never squash, stretch, duplicate, detach, or float structural parts. Use thick magenta gutters so no sprite enters a neighboring cell.
