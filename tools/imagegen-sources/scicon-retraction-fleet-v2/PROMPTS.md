# Scicon Retraction Fleet v2 — ImageGen prompts

Mode: OpenAI built-in image generation. The attached live-game screenshot was
used only for compact gameplay scale; the active red lower-mission interceptor
and the first generated family were used only as style references.

All five prompts required exactly three registered top-down poses on a flat
magenta chroma field, authentic chunky 32-bit pixel art, a shared
red/gunmetal/cyan faction language, and a compact 40–48 pixel gameplay read.
Long needles, spears, narrow vertical hulls, health bars, warning circles,
projectiles, text, and UI were explicitly prohibited.

1. **Redline Skirmisher** — broad clipped arrowhead scout with short cannons and
   maneuver ports; its silhouette communicates fast lateral dodges.
2. **Crosscut Raider** — compact X/bat interceptor with four short scissor wings
   that open through the charge sequence for paired-flank attacks.
3. **Audit Bulwark** — squat hexagonal heavy whose layered side plates visibly
   brace into a shield stance rather than relying on a floating health bar.
4. **Quasar Lancer v2** — broad lane-laser specialist with short horizontal
   emitter rails; the emitted light is the lance, not the hull.
5. **Vector Reaver v2** — compact wide scissor ace with hooked wings and a
   notched front edge, avoiding both needle and horseshoe silhouettes.
