# Assumptions, limitations and evidence status

The current result is a research platform with independently counted real anatomy and tested numerical mechanisms. It does not establish that a fly brain sees the game or learns to play it. Reports default to **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**.

An initial frozen, real-data candidate assay failed: 1,767 L1 neurons with actual optic-hex annotations received an explicitly crude planar projection of six 50-ms stimuli. Illumination produced 10,602 L1 spikes but zero response in 335 nominated visual KCs and 97 MBONs; all decoder outputs were idle/no-fire. All driven L1 cells have negative modeled fast-transmission sign. In a quiescent network with no tonic excitation, their inhibitory output cannot recruit undriven cells. This is a failure of this engineered interface/model combination, not evidence against fly visual capability. Inputs, thresholds and responses are preserved in [the evidence record](docs/evidence/malecns-hex-v0.1-screen.json).

- Anatomical contact counts are not measured conductance. The sign map, global gain, delay, time constants, background drive and reset rules are model assumptions. ACh is positive and GABA/Glu/histamine negative in this baseline; unsupported/modulatory transmitters have zero fast-transmission sign while retaining anatomical edges. Receptors, cotransmission, extrasynaptic effects and heterogeneous physiology are not reconstructed.
- All-LIF is a numerical reference, particularly limited for graded early vision. Matching Brian2 verifies equations and scheduling, not biology. The published Shiu FlyWire behavioral assay has not been reproduced on MaleCNS here.
- Missing retinal cells and incomplete lamina coverage require an engineered sensory boundary. Soma coordinates are not pixels. The retained optic annotations do not by themselves validate a retinal map, visual KC pathway or meaningful natural-scene encoding.
- The action decoder is an engineered game effector. Firing has no established fly-specific circuit. Keeping VNC neurons in memory does not demonstrate VNC contribution; cell/pathway interventions must establish it.
- Signed scalar reinforcement is imposed numerically on selected DANs. It is not real dopamine. The plasticity rule and its spike-bin adaptation are hypotheses. Full-graph training is blocked until identity-bound sensory, numerical, decoder and conditioning validation exists.
- Checkpoint restore is exact state restoration within a model/runtime, not a retention assay. Analytical passive-memory retention explicitly omits whole-network sleep dynamics. Cross-platform floating-point or cross-GPU pixel equality is not promised.
- The sealed ORBIT ENTRY schedule tests seed robustness. Unseen scenario transfer, reversal studies and all-CNS functional claims need their own frozen protocols. No positive biological study result is supplied.
- The CPU reference benchmark is too slow for the default pilot on this computer. Local D3D11 materially improves game capture, but does not accelerate neural equations. GPU/event-driven optimization must be checked against the reference before large studies.
- Browser validation covers a bounded portion of the mission. Existing engine regressions protect normal desktop/mobile input, damage and progression; this is not a claim to have exercised every authored wave, ship, asset or device. Exact second-machine reproduction still requires that installation and its runtime/asset verification.
- The neural filesystem boundary prevents accidental access in trusted Python, not malicious native extensions under the same Windows account. Production services are excluded by architecture, sanitized environments, loopback binding and blocked game-browser requests; external attacker isolation would require an OS sandbox.
- A report can be complete while its evidence is negative or insufficient. Missing models, failed gates, corrupt checkpoints, asset/version mismatch and interrupted runs must remain visible rather than converted to plausible-looking activity or scores.
