## Identity

- Fantasy: a frontier bastion crab whose armored shell, crown reactor, and chain-launched claws behave like one deliberate siege mechanism.
- Combat role: a heavy anatomy-reading route boss that alternates locked harpoon dodges, reactor-zone decisions, and lateral formation routing before combining the taught tether rules in a signature puzzle.
- Silhouette: a low, wide teal shell; large native-handed primary claws; complete rear and upper appendages; six small bracing legs; gold winches; and one centered energy-blue crown reactor.
- Unique mechanism: each primary claw remains visibly connected to a hull winch by repeated pixel chain links sampled between a live hull socket and a live claw socket through launch, tension, impact, retraction, and reseating.
- Combat sentence: read the mechanism that is winding up, move through its authored opening, then punish the exposed crown reactor while the heavy rig mechanically recovers.
- Test-only status: yes. Promotion is outside this task.

## Player learning arc

- Skill A - commitment reading: identify a claw's posture and locked target before its visible tethered launch, dodge the committed strike, and respect only visibly active claw/chain damage.
- Skill B - region timing: read the crown aperture's pulse sequence, occupy the unlit region, then cross only after the active beam edge visibly dissipates.
- Skill C - movement prediction: read crabwalk direction, active rear appendage, and the missing slot in each authored barrage formation while accounting for the moving hull.
- Signature puzzle - Crown Cross-Link: follow a rotating hinged opening between two nonparallel, visibly tensioned tethers as one claw pays out and the other retracts. This is not a parallel projectile corridor and never becomes an invisible clamp.
- Recovery reward: successful solutions end with the crown aperture open, chain spools stopped, claws settling, and the reactor vulnerable for a deliberate punish window.

## Native pixel-art contract

- Accepted source/runtime sizes: the native source neutral is `224 x 192` px and the registered binary-alpha runtime neutral is `260 x 210` px. Alternate frames for one part use identical canvases, registration, handedness, pivot, and scale sign.
- Display intent: aspect-preserved nearest-neighbor scaling. Target roughly 58-62% of the mobile arena width without covering the route below the boss; final display dimensions are Gate 2 decisions, not design assumptions.
- Palette target: 32 or fewer structural colors across teal armor, steel shadows, gold mechanisms, energy-blue focal light, and outline ramps. VFX may use a small separate translucent palette.
- Structural alpha: binary. Semitransparency belongs only to glow, beam, impact, sparks, and other separate effects.
- Outline: one- or two-pixel dark structural outline with larger readable clusters; no antialiased structural edges or soft painted gradients.
- Detail rule: preserve large shell panels, crown/reactor shape, joint plates, chain housings, and primary claw teeth; consolidate tiny illustration noise into two- to four-pixel clusters.
- Scaling rule: all structural parts scale uniformly with the root. No independent claw shrink, nonuniform stretch, or hidden double mirror is allowed.

## Accepted neutral corrections

1. Rebuild the complete intact silhouette at native gameplay resolution; do not blindly downscale the current semi-pixel illustration.
2. Preserve the broad shell-to-claw ratio and make each primary claw a dominant mobile-readable mass rather than a small projectile attachment.

5. Place shoulder plates, telescoping connectors, wrist caps, and hull winches so every appendage overlaps its parent through the full validated range.

## Purposeful animation and lifecycle

### Entrance

The folded shell descends heavily. Legs unfold in offset pairs, rear claws clear the silhouette, the primary claws lower into their wrists, both winches take up slack, and the reactor performs one blue boot pulse before the neutral idle begins.

### Idle

- Root: slow hover and a very small delayed shell tilt, never constant shake.
- Reactor: a two-frame idle family switched every six simulation frames with a brief blink, plus a separate four-frame charge family; it is the primary frame-animated focal component.
- Shell/crown: restrained vent and crown-fin shift at a slower cadence than the reactor.
- Legs: small offset two- or three-frame articulation; middle legs lag front and rear pairs.
- Rear appendages: slight open/close delay following body sway.
- Primary claws: heavy one-pixel settle and occasional jaw flex while remaining seated.
- Winches: stopped in neutral. They rotate only during launch/retraction and visibly settle after reseating.

### Attack motion

- Harpoon: brace, spool, claw-open, socket flash, launch, tension, impact/close, recoil, retract, reseat, settle.
- Reactor beam: leg brace, shell/crown open, aperture charge frames, startup flash, active beam, dissipation, recovery flicker, close.
- Crabwalk: alternating leg frames, slight body roll into travel, rear appendage opening, per-volley recoil, readable boundary turn, deceleration, settle.
- Cross-Link: unique dual-spool and dual-claw frames, opposite chain motion, crown synchronization pulse, heavy dual reseat, exposed-core recovery.

### Hit, stagger, damage, transition, and destruction

- Ordinary hit: local two- or three-frame reactor/shell flash, one or two sprite sparks, and a tiny appendage reaction. No whole-rig flinch.
- Large hit: brief directional root recoil with local claw and rear-appendage lag, reserved for meaningful damage.
- Stagger: claws lose tension and lower, winches stop, legs tuck/brace, shell drops, and the crown aperture remains exposed.
- Persistent damage: native-pixel crack overlays, small electric-zap and tiny-explosion sprite families, uneven reactor pulse, and one visibly rough mechanism. Effects are bounded and cannot obscure projectiles.
- Phase transition: described in Critical Cross-Link below; it is a persistent anatomy state, not a flash.
- Death: winches fail, chain tension releases, claws droop, small armor fragments detach, rear appendages collapse, gold mechanisms spark, shell panels buckle, reactor overloads, large claw/shell fragments separate, then one final blue-gold eruption clears every hostile entity and chain segment deterministically.

## Exactly three core attack sentences

### Attack 1 - Chainclaw Harpoon (core)

- Player question: which committed claw target should I leave first, and where can I stand while the second claw takes its related but different route?
- Owning anatomy: primary claws, matching shoulder/wrist joints, hull and claw chain sockets, and matching winches.
- Recognition cue: the rig braces toward one side, the chosen winch begins rotating, the matching claw opens, and energy gathers inside the claw before a short target-lock hold records the ship position.
- Bounded branch: fixed seed chooses `port-then-starboard` or `starboard-then-port`; the second strike is `shallow-then-deep` or `diagonal-then-near-vertical`. The visible pose identifies the branch before damage.
- Safe rule: leave the warning-locked ship position, remain clear of the full-size open claw as it travels deep toward that committed row, avoid the attached chain while it is latched or visibly tensioned, then cross only after the chain becomes non-damaging for retraction. The strike never tracks a later ship movement after its warning lock.
- Connected beats: upper-center settle -> six-leg brace -> first spool/start and `claw.open` warning -> ship-position lock -> full-size launched claw and `chain.segment` payout toward the locked lower depth -> latched/tensioned chain damage -> impact recoil -> non-damaging retract/reseat -> opposite claw warning/lock/launch with a different depth -> impact/retract -> dual mechanical settle.
- Recoil: the owning shoulder compresses, the shell rolls slightly toward the pulling chain, and the corresponding rear appendage braces.
- Recovery: both claws seat with one heavy snap; winches stop; crown aperture opens briefly for a punish window; every transform returns exactly to neutral.
- Destroyed-claw fallback: omit every hidden/destroyed side socket, chain, collision, and animation immediately. One surviving claw owns both Harpoon beats. With both claws gone, even action sequences substitute Crown Reactor Beam and odd sequences substitute Bastion Crabwalk Barrage.

### Attack 2 - Crown Reactor Beam (core)

- Player question: which region is about to become active, and when can I cross behind the one controlled beam shift?
- Owning anatomy: crown fins, shell panels, reactor aperture/core, six bracing legs, and the live reactor beam socket.
- Recognition cue: the boss stabilizes, legs lock, crown fins open in registered frames, gold mechanisms rotate inward, and two short nondamaging blue pulses establish the first active region.
- Bounded branch: fixed seed chooses a left or right first active lane and one adjacent controlled shift; phase two may reverse once, but the second crown pulse announces that reversal before the beam moves.
- Safe rule: occupy the unlit region shown by the aperture pulses, then cross behind the visibly dissipating beam edge during the authored shift. At least one arena region remains open at all times.
- Connected beats: stable firing position -> brace -> three-stage reactor charge -> short rule pulse -> startup flash -> first active beam -> controlled shift or second beam beat -> dissipation -> recovery flicker -> crown close.
- Recoil: shell panels compress, rear appendages brace inward, and the root receives one small heavy kick opposite the beam.
- Recovery: aperture flickers and remains exposed while legs release one pair at a time; beam collision is disabled before the dissipation art clears.

### Attack 3 - Bastion Crabwalk Barrage (core)

- Player question: which direction is the armored crab committing to, which rear appendage owns the next volley, and where is the authored missing slot?
- Owning anatomy: all six locomotion legs, shell tilt, rear/upper claws, and the live port/starboard rear-emitter sockets.
- Recognition cue: legs accelerate in alternating pairs, the shell leans into travel, and only the rear appendage that owns the next formation opens and glows.
- Bounded branch: fixed seed chooses left-to-right or right-to-left first, plus one of two named formation templates. The boss reverses once at a visible arena boundary and the active rear appendage swaps.
- Safe rule: stay off the moving hull, move through the visibly missing slot in each serialized formation, then change sides only after the boss's boundary brace announces its reversal. This is a sequence of authored formations, not continuous gunfire or radial spam.
- Connected beats: leg wake-up -> lateral acceleration -> rear-port or rear-starboard open -> first staggered arc -> second formation with one shifted aperture -> boundary brace -> readable direction reversal -> opposite rear emitter formation -> final crown-synchronized bolt pair -> deceleration -> leg and shell settle.
- Recoil: each rear volley rolls its appendage and the hull by a small registered amount; the boundary turn plants all six legs before direction changes.
- Recovery: boss returns to its standard upper position, rear emitters close, legs stop in neutral registration, and the reactor is punishable during shell settling.

## Signature - Crown Cross-Link

- Kind: signature identity, not a fourth core attack.
- Player question: can I follow the rotating hinged doorway made by two visible tethers while their claws move at different rates?
- Recognition cue: Crownclaw moves to upper center, both claws open to unique frames, both winches expose blue tension coils, and the reactor sends one synchronized pulse into both sockets.
- Bounded branch: fixed seed chooses clockwise or counterclockwise opening rotation. Later complete uses alternate direction; no unrestricted randomness is allowed.
- Safe rule: two claws launch to opposite side anchors, creating nonparallel chain boundaries with one deliberate gap near their separated endpoints. One winch pays out as the other retracts so the gap rotates continuously; after a clear crossover pause, the relationship reverses once. The player follows the hinged opening rather than camping an outer gutter or entering a parallel bullet corridor.
- Connected beats: center settle -> dual brace/open -> dual spool warning -> opposite-side launches -> visible tension wave establishes active boundaries -> coordinated payout/retraction rotates the opening -> one claw closes as the other yields a crossover window -> one readable reversal -> heavy dual retract/reseat -> reactor exposure.
- Damage contract: an actively launched open claw is damaging through its launching, latched, and tensioned windows. Chain collision is enabled during the latched and tensioned windows and follows the same `chain.segment` path drawn between live sockets. Warning, initial payout, retraction, reseat, recovery, and slack chain art are non-damaging.
- Peak: both claws, both chain paths, winches, crown aperture, rear braces, and shell tilt participate in a unique registered key pose.
- Recovery: both chains straighten during retraction, claws reconnect with a heavy mechanical snap, the shell sags, and the crown reactor remains fully exposed longer than after a core attack.
- Distinction from Helios: hazards are articulated nonparallel tethers and moving claw anchors; there are no mounted machine-gun rails, continuous projectile streams, mines, or clamp closure.

## Phase two - Critical Cross-Link

Critical Cross-Link begins at the authored health threshold only after the active attack cleans up.

Persistent anatomy change:

1. All six legs brace and the boss drops slightly.
2. Rear claws rotate outward and remain more open.
3. Primary claws lock open while the winch housings split to reveal energy-blue tension coils.
4. Crown fins separate and the reactor aperture remains visibly exposed between attacks.
5. Two small shell plates break away, revealing gold cross-link braces beneath the teal shell.
6. One rear mechanism adopts a slight damaged lag while the opposite side braces harder; the silhouette becomes deliberately asymmetric without losing handedness.

Behavioral remix:

- Chainclaw Harpoon coordinates the two taught commitment depths with a visible handoff; it never adds an untelegraphed target change.
- Crown Reactor Beam may perform one clearly pulsed reversal and uses the permanently open aperture to shorten only the visual opening step, not the player's warning contract.
- Bastion Crabwalk Barrage traverses a broader but still bounded range and selects the second retained aperture template after the visible boundary brace.
- Crown Cross-Link can energize one chain at a time before both become active, making the same rotating-door solution more coordinated rather than merely faster.
- Recovery changes are anatomical: the exposed reactor is easier to punish, while the open winch housings make chain preparation easier to read.

## Fight grammar

`Entrance -> teach Chainclaw Harpoon -> teach Bastion Crabwalk Barrage -> teach Crown Reactor Beam -> vary Harpoon -> vary Crabwalk -> Crown Cross-Link -> exposed-reactor recovery -> Critical Cross-Link transition -> phase-two Beam remix -> phase-two Crabwalk remix -> phase-two Cross-Link -> stagger -> staged destruction`

- Prevent excessive repeats with deterministic bounded selection.
- Do not place the first Harpoon lesson immediately before the full signature.
- Any attack reconstruction, phase jump, restart, or owner loss must remove claws-in-flight, chains, beam collision, projectiles, warnings, and transient VFX before the next state begins.
